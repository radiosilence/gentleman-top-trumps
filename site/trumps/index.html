The Deck

How to play

  1. The deck is shuffled and dealt between you and your opponent. You only ever see the top card of your own pile.
  2. Whoever holds the lead picks a category from their card. Both cards are compared on it.
  3. The higher number wins both cards, which go to the bottom of the winner's pile, and the winner takes the lead.
  4. A tie sends both cards to the pot. The same player picks again, and the next hand's winner takes the pot as well.
  5. Take every card to win.
